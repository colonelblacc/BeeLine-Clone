## Pairing Flow

Source: https://support.beeline.co/en/articles/9389503-pairing-with-moto-ii
IMPORTANT: Do NOT pair via phone Bluetooth settings. Must pair through the Beeline app only.

Steps:
1. Hold bottom button 3 seconds to turn on Moto II
2. Device shows 4-character ID (e.g. AB12) in white box at bottom of screen
3. Open Beeline app
4. First time: onboarding prompts Connect a Beeline device
5. Returning: Settings > Beeline device > Pair
6. Select device from list, allow pairing prompts
7. Moto II downloads firmware update automatically
8. Device ready to ride

Unpairing:
- Via app: Settings > Unpair
- Via device: Options menu > Unpair (when phone unavailable)
- After unpair: Forget device in phone Bluetooth settings before re-pairing (critical on iOS)
